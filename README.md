Arma 3 Realism Overhaul, 'A3RO' for short, aims to achieve realism in aspects not covered by other modifications as well as improving on existing ones, all the while maintaining the highest possible level of backwards compatibility, featuring native support for CDLC and other Modifications.

A3RO is also available for download on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?edit=true&id=3341786920).
[![A3-RO-Overhauled-Compressed.gif](https://i.postimg.cc/tgQ4gms9/A3-RO-Overhauled-Compressed.gif)](https://postimg.cc/4YPgLPv0)
## Core Features
Overhaul Summary as of Version 1.1.5:  

Ammo, Tracers, Magazines, Weapons, UI Icons, Attachments, Explosives, Items, Backpacks
#### Ammo Overhaul
All Infantry Ammo has been re-evalued, with a new, standardized configuration applied across the board.
With ACE loaded, Ballistics Simulation is taken to the next level. Every Ammo Type has received updates to its ACE Config where possible.
#### Tracers Overhaul
Tracers Ignite and Extinguish correctly. Tracer Size Scaling has been standardized.
#### Magazines Overhaul
Tracer Ammo availablity has been standardized. Tracer Mix availability depends on Magazine Size.
You will have the option between: No Tracers, Reload Tracer, Tracer Every 4, and Full Tracer. IR Tracers are available for Calibres where they exist.
For this, hundreds of Magazines and over 600 custom Icons have been created to fill the gaps throughout the Game.
Every Magazine features a Description, aswell as an Abbreviation to be displayed in the UI where required.
Belts are Heavy.
#### Weapons Overhaul
Every conceivable aspect of Infantry Weapons has been analyzed, researched and adjusted to achieve the most accurate representation possible.
Countless Inconsistencies and incorrect Attachment Slots have been fixed, replaced, and hidden ones added.
Binoculars, Rangefinders and Laser Designators have never been better.
#### UI Icons Overhaul
The Position and Scale of Attachments displayed on any weapon in the Inventory is now accurate.
Weapon, Magazine and Item Icons of existing Items have seen a refurbishment where desperately needed.
#### Attachments Overhaul
In the same manner, every type of Weapon Attachment has been reconfigured to represent its real life counterpart.
#### Explosives Overhaul
From every Handgrenade, over to Mines and Explosives Charges, to Underbarrel Grenades; They have all seen a complete revision of their performance and functionality.
This is the only complete implementation in Arma that ensures a distinction between available UGL Grenades between the factions.
#### Item Overhaul
All other Items you would find in the Arsenal have seen changes. Be it your wristwatch, DAGR, Toolkit, or all the other Items added by ACE.
#### Backpacks Overhaul
All Load Bearing Backpacks have had their capacity increased to balance the increased burden of realistic Equipment, and their weight corrected.
Statics Backpacks and some other functional Backpacks have also been modified.
#### New Content
With Assets set in order, they can be expanded upon. New civilian Sporting Variants of existing Weapons, new ammo types, and hidden content such as weapon attachments or skins have been added from the gamefiles.
Meticulous care was placed on Texture work for the new weapon variants.
#### Maximum Compatibility
No stone left unturned. A3RO is intended to apply all changes across the entire game. That means: No missing Magazines in Units, no missing Editor Objects, no Error Messages or Bugs during Gameplay.
Vanilla Missions are currently unsupported, as they cannot be modified via Configuration. Most will work, however some utilize custom Loadouts that may be incompatible with A3RO. A script to replace affected Items on runtime will be developed.

#### ACE 3
Loading ACE3 will apply all A3RO Overhauls onto ACE content. In turn, ACE configuration is applied to all classes edited by A3RO where possible in order to create the most realistic version of Arma 3 yet.
#### ACE_Tracers
Loading ACE_Tracers will apply ACE_Tracer models to all ammo added by A3RO. Furthermore, ACE_Tracers benefits from A3ROs Tracer Scale Standardization.
#### ACEAX
Loading ACEAX will enable A3ROs custom categorization of all Weapons. Other ACEAX for supported Addons can be run alongside to sort content unaffected by A3RO.
#### Western Sahara CDLC
Loading the Western Sahara CDLC will apply all A3RO Overhauls to the DLC content where possible. Further derivative content becomes available for both DLC and other Assets.
#### Real Engine Enhanced
Loading Real Engine Enhanced will overwrite affected weapon configuration with A3RO values for a seamless integration of both mods.
## Dependencies
The only Mod required to play with A3RO is [CBA_A3](https://github.com/CBATeam/CBA_A3).  
CBA_A3 provides vital functionality and intercompatibility options that allow A3RO to be easily applied onto other Addons.
